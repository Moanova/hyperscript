# Hyperscript :: Vision produit


## Hyperscript : A visual narrative map editor for modern fiction writers.
- **Description**:
    Hyperscript is the application that enables writers of video games, micro-series and other forms of modern fiction to
      - visually build their narrative maps without any technical skills,
      - manage branching paths and narrative arcs with a clear view of the links between elements,
      - export their work into universal formats to collaborate with their teams (developers, directors) without manual operations.
    Why?
    Standard word-processing software is ill-suited for this purpose, while specialized professional tools often prove too complex or too expensive.
    Existing solutions do not fully meet the needs of modern fiction writers, especially independent creators.
    1. Flexibility: suited to complex, non-linear narratives.
    2. Simplicity: an intuitive, code-free interface so you can focus on structuring your stories.
    3. Collaboration: simplified sharing with teams via directly usable exports.
    In a nutshell, Hyperscript is the visual Swiss Army knife for independent writers, enabling them to structure complex narratives without getting bogged down in technicalities.
